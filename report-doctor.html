Import Report Doctor

Drop in a BlueCollar import_report.json file and get a plain-language readout of what the importer did, where fidelity may have degraded, and what to try next.

Everything runs locally in your browser. The report and PDF are not uploaded.

Load A Report

Use the report path shown after import, or open the newest import_report.json / last_import.log companion folder from the importer.

What It Checks

  • Host/importer identity and version clues
  • Auto, Vector, Raster, or Hybrid strategy evidence
  • Fallbacks and rasterized pages
  • Requested text mode and actual text-output clues
  • Text Entity Verification (requested vs. observed entity types)
  • Ready Check results and repair hints (paste a ready_check.json)
  • Geometry, image, page, and layer counts
  • Performance timing, hints, and helper/renderer details
  • Support-ready summary text

No magic claims

This page explains importer telemetry. It does not certify engineering correctness, replace manual review, or inspect the original PDF.
