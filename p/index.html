Part not published. This tag identifies a piece mark from a fabrication drawing. Published fields (when the fabricator opts in) appear here as static JSON; drawing data is never uploaded by default.

Tags like this are made with Steel Logic — field tools for fabricators. Open the app for the full editable record, or paste your local import_report.json into the Report Doctor.

Tag data lives in the parts_bootstrap.json sidecar your importer wrote next to the drawing. Optional public mirrors use /p-records/<id>.json (bcs.part/1.0).

Open this part

  • In Steel Logic: open in the app — resolves the mark against your local sidecar and drawing.
  • No app? Open your import_report.json in the Report Doctor and use the Tags tab to see every mark on the drawing.
  • Get the tools: importers and the Steel Logic app are on the downloads page.

Nothing about your drawing is sent to this server unless the fabricator explicitly publishes a /p-records/<id>.json record. This page only reads the part ID from the address bar and optional static JSON.
